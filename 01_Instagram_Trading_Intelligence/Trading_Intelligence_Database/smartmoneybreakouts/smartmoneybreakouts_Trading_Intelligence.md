> 📊 VWAP EXPLAINED — PART 7/10
>
> 🔥 VWAP + RSI: Momentum Confirmation
>
> VWAP helps you understand intraday direction, while RSI helps you measure momentum.
>
> Combining both can help you identify whether buyers or sellers are actually showing strength behind a move.
>
> 🟢 BULLISH SETUP
>
> Look for:
>
> ✅ Price above VWAP
>
> ✅ Price above 20 EMA & 50 EMA
>
> ✅ RSI above 50
>
> ✅ Higher highs & higher lows
>
> ✅ Volume supporting the upward move
>
> 🎯 Entry: After bullish price-action confirmation near VWAP or EMA support.
>
> 🔴 BEARISH SETUP
>
> Look for:
>
> ✅ Price below VWAP
>
> ✅ Price below 20 EMA & 50 EMA
>
> ✅ RSI below 50
>
> ✅ Lower highs & lower lows
>
> ✅ Volume supporting the downward move
>
> 🎯 Entry: After bearish price-action confirmation near VWAP or EMA resistance.
>
> 📌 RSI SETTINGS
>
> • RSI Period: 14
>
> • Bullish momentum: Above 50
>
> • Bearish momentum: Below 50
>
> 💡 IMPORTANT
>
> Don’t buy just because RSI crosses above 50 or sell just because it falls below 50.
>
> Always combine RSI with:
>
> Trend + VWAP + EMA + Price Action + Volume.
>
> YOUR TRADING FRAMEWORK
>
> Trend → VWAP → EMA → RSI → Confirmation → Entry → SL → Targets

### Setup #12: 7 EMA INTRADAY STRATEGY — PART 3/3

- **Target Asset / Market:** `Equities & Financial Markets`
- **Trading Domain:** `Day Trading / Intraday, Options Trading, Quantitative & Algorithmic, Risk Management`
- **Key Indicators & Tools:** `EMA, VWAP`
- **Format:** `Carousel (Sidecar)`

#### Written Technical Rules & Analysis:

> 📉 7 EMA INTRADAY STRATEGY — PART 3/3
>
> ⚠️ When the 7 EMA Fails: 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid
>
> The 7 EMA can help you identify short-term momentum, but blindly following it can lead to unnecessary losses. 📊
>
> Before using this strategy in intraday trading, understand these 5 common mistakes.
>
> ❌ 1. Trading Every EMA Touch
>
> Price can touch the 7 EMA multiple times without continuing in the expected direction. Wait for a suitable setup and confirmation.
>
> ❌ 2. Trading in Sideways Markets
>
> When price moves sideways, the 7 EMA can produce repeated false signals. Wait for a clearer trend instead of forcing trades.
>
> ❌ 3. Entering Without Candle Confirmation
>
> An EMA touch alone is not an entry signal. Look for a confirmation candle and check whether the overall price structure supports your trade.
>
> ❌ 4. Ignoring Support & Resistance
>
> A bullish setup near strong resistance or a bearish setup near strong support may have limited room to move. Always check nearby key levels.
>
> ❌ 5. Poor Risk Management
>
> Even a good setup can fail. Define your stop-loss before entering, calculate your position size, and plan your exit.
>
> ✅ Additional Tips for Intraday Traders
>
> • Check the higher-timeframe trend.
>
> • Use VWAP, volume and key levels as supporting tools.
>
> • Be cautious around major news events.
>
> • Maintain a trading journal and review your results.
>
> • Backtest your rules before risking real money.
>
> 🎯 Remember: The 7 EMA is a tool, not a guarantee. It works best as part of a complete trading plan—not as a standalone buy or sell signal.
