- **Key Indicators & Tools:** `Price Action & Market Structure`
- **Format:** `Carousel (Sidecar)`

#### Written Technical Rules & Analysis:

> Liquidity is the fuel that drives every market move. Institutions don’t buy or sell randomly—they build positions where the most pending orders sit. These are liquidity pools formed by retail stop-losses above highs, below lows, and around obvious trendlines. Price often sweeps these levels first, triggering retail stops and collecting enough orders for the next directional move. Understanding this explains why clean support or resistance levels break so often.
>
> To trade with smart money, track where traders feel ‘safe’—tight ranges, neat double tops or bottoms, and heavily watched trendlines. Those areas attract both stop orders and breakout traders, creating the perfect trap. When prices raid those zones and then snap back, that’s usually the real entry for institutions.
>
> Learn to read these sweeps and you stop chasing fake breakouts and start positioning with the players who move the market.
