### Setup #12: Trading consistency comes from mastering one trading strategy, no

- **Target Asset / Market:** `Equities & Financial Markets`
- **Trading Domain:** `Quantitative & Algorithmic, Trading Psychology, Risk Management`
- **Key Indicators & Tools:** `Price Action & Market Structure`
- **Format:** `Single Photo`

#### Written Technical Rules & Analysis:

> Trading consistency comes from mastering one trading strategy, not switching between many. 📈
>
> Many traders jump from strategy to strategy: a new indicator, a new setup, a new YouTube system. Every losing week feels like proof the strategy failed, so they start over again. But no trading strategy wins every week. 🔁
>
> Focus is a real edge in trading. When you stick to one setup, you learn how it behaves in trending and ranging markets, how to manage risk with it, and when to stay out. That depth builds confidence, discipline and consistent results. 🎯
>
> Backtest it. Journal every trade. Give it time before you judge it.
